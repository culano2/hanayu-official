# HANAYU Logo Guidelines

## Primary Lockup

The primary wordmark is `HANAYU` with `Huayu Island` as the origin line. The wordmark should remain letterspaced, calm, and premium.

## Usage

- Use the light logo on Deep Ocean, Midnight Blue, photography, and dark overlays.
- Use the dark logo only on Pearl White or Mist Gray backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the origin line.
- Keep the logo aligned to strong layout edges.

## Do Not

- Do not compress, stretch, rotate, or outline the wordmark.
- Do not place the logo over busy imagery without an overlay.
- Do not change the origin line to marketing copy.
- Do not apply gradients or heavy effects to the logo.
