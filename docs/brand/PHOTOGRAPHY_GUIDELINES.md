# HANAYU Photography Guidelines

## Direction

Photography should feel like premium documentary work: quiet, natural, textured, and place-driven. Images should show the actual sea, harbor, craft, product, or packaging whenever possible.

## Preferred Subjects

- Huayu Island coastline and cliffs.
- Fishing boats, harbor preparation, and night fishing.
- Product details with natural cold texture.
- Ocean surfaces, rocks, and underwater light.
- Premium packaging in restrained compositions.

## Treatment

- Use deep contrast with visible detail.
- Add dark overlays only when needed for text readability.
- Keep color grading natural, with navy, slate, pearl, and gold accents.
- Avoid stock-like blur, fake lens flare, cartoon styling, or overly saturated seafood imagery.

## Accessibility

Every meaningful image must include alt text that describes the subject and context. Decorative overlays should remain hidden from assistive technology.
