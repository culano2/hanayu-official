# HANAYU Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence

HANAYU is a premium Taiwanese seafood brand from the waters around Huayu Island, Penghu. The brand should feel quiet, precise, seasonal, and deeply connected to place.

## Official Messaging

- Brand Tagline: 把海鮮，做成精品。
- Brand Promise: 一年只有一季，值得等待。
- Craft Message: 每一尾，都由職人親手釣起。
- Brand Position: 台灣精品海鮮品牌，來自澎湖花嶼海域。

## Voice

Use restrained, confident language. Avoid exaggerated luxury claims, urgency-driven sales copy, or generic seafood marketing. The strongest HANAYU copy should feel like an origin note, not a sales pitch.

## Experience Principles

- Lead with origin, craft, and seasonality.
- Keep whitespace generous.
- Use documentary imagery with natural texture.
- Let premium detail come from restraint, not decoration.
- Keep the dark navy and gold identity consistent.
